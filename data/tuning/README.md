# data/tuning

Numbers that shape play. Every record is labeled either `sourced` (with a `source_id` from `docs/research/SOURCES.md`) or `gameplay_tuned` (a design choice that is never presented as a historical statistic).

- `daily_life.json`: how needs rise and fall, when people talk, sit out, play or drop in at the store, and how conversations change regard.
- `economy.json`: the meal price, how many meals a person eats, when the pantry counts as low and how far a trip restocks it, how long shopping takes, the youngest age sent shopping, how much work counts as a day worked, and how little a thin meal does for hunger.

Every value now in these files is `gameplay_tuned`. The money figures are invented for play on the fictional test street; none is a historical price, wage or rent.
