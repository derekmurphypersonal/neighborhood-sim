# Candidate Blocks for the First Playable Area

Status: 2026-11-13. **Decided: Derek chose Maynard Street, at its Oliver Street end (D-037 in [DECISIONS.md](../../DECISIONS.md)).** Maynard St. lies one block south of candidate 1 below, on the same sheet; what the map shows there is finding F-058. The candidates are kept as the record of what was considered, and candidate 1's school, church and library are the nearest places for Maynard St. residents to go.

Everything below rests on findings F-051 to F-057 in [FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md), which are `probable`: read by Claude from medium-resolution images of the Sanborn volume corrected to March 1951, and not yet confirmed by a person or against full-resolution images. No game data has been entered from them.

## Where Linden is on the maps

The 1951 volume's index marks streets as "at Linden" and places them on **sheets 62 and 70**, and both sheets carry the label "LINDEN". The 1957 volume covers the same ground on its sheets 62, 70 and 20. Working copies are in `research/sanborn/` at the repository root.

## Candidates

### 1. The school and firehouse blocks (sheet 62, Sanborn blocks 22 and 23)

Bounded by Clapp St., Oliver St. and Bellvale St., with Springdale St. between the two blocks.

- **On it, as read:** "Fire Dept. Hose Co." with a "Branch Library"; "Linden Grammar School (Built 1889)"; "Linden M.E. Church"; dwellings of 2 to 2½ stories along Clapp, Springdale and Bellvale; a row marked "11 auto stalls".
- **For:** mostly dwellings with four institutions residents would use every day (school, church, library, firehouse), which gives a week of play places to go without needing any business names. Street numbers are printed on the sheet. The 1957 sheet shows the same blocks, so change over the decade can be checked, and the school appears to have been renamed by 1957.
- **Against:** no shops on the block itself. The two blocks together hold more than the ten to twenty buildings the selection criteria aim for; the east end of each (the part nearest Oliver St.) would be about the right size.

### 2. The store cluster by the railroad (sheet 62, around Sanborn block 8)

Where Lynn St. and Beach St. meet beside the railroad, with Hancock Rd. and Munroe St. behind.

- **On it, as read:** a group of buildings marked as stores, one marked "Rest.", a filling station, an auto body works across the tracks, "St. Luke's Episc'l Church" a short way along Lynn St., and dwellings behind.
- **For:** the commercial heart of the neighborhood as the map shows it, beside the railroad, which ties in with the reported end of passenger trains in 1958 (lead F-013). Likely the "Linden Square" of other sources, though the sheet does not say so.
- **Against:** the sheet gives uses, not names, so this block depends on a 1950s street directory that has not been found. In the 1957 volume this corner appears to move to sheet 20, which makes the two editions harder to compare. The passenger station has not been located.

### 3. The Lawrence St. / Oliver St. rows north of Beach St. (sheet 70)

- **On it, as read:** long, regular rows of dwellings on Lawrence, Oliver and Wesmur Rd.; Linden Brook; an industrial site ("Hawkridge Bros Co", steel) beside the railroad at the edge.
- **For:** a plain residential street with a nearby workplace.
- **Against:** no institutions or shops; least to do in a week.

## Suggested order

Candidate 1 first, because it can be built from the map alone and has places for residents to go. Candidate 2 is the better long-term centre of the game but should wait for a directory. This is a suggestion for Q-001, not a decision.
